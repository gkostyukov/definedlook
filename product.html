Hydrating Serum

Our signature Hydrating Serum is a lightweight, fast-absorbing formula that delivers intense moisture deep into your skin. Powered by hyaluronic acid and botanical extracts, this serum helps plump, smooth, and restore your skin's natural radiance.

  • ✓ Suitable for all skin types
  • ✓ Hyaluronic acid for deep hydration
  • ✓ Vitamin B5 to soothe and repair
  • ✓ Non-comedogenic formula
  • ✓ Cruelty-free and vegan

SKU: DL-HS-001

Category: Serums

Tags: Hydration, Anti-Aging, Daily Care

Key Ingredients

Hyaluronic Acid: A powerful humectant that can hold up to 1000x its weight in water, providing deep hydration and plumping effects.

Vitamin B5 (Panthenol): Helps to soothe, moisturize, and repair the skin barrier.

Niacinamide: Improves skin texture, minimizes pores, and evens out skin tone.

Botanical Extracts: Green tea and chamomile extracts provide antioxidant protection and calming benefits.

How to Use

  1. Cleanse your face thoroughly and pat dry.
  2. Apply 2-3 drops of serum to your fingertips.
  3. Gently press the serum into your face and neck, avoiding the eye area.
  4. Follow with your favorite moisturizer.
  5. Use morning and evening for best results.

Pro Tip: Apply to damp skin for enhanced absorption and hydration.
